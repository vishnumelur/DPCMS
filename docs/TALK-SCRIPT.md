## Q&A — DEFENCE ANSWERS (have these ready)

**"It runs on free hosting / connectors are simulated — is this production-ready?"**
"The demo runs on free infrastructure deliberately, so you can verify every claim today at zero risk. For production we move to India-region, MeitY-empanelled hosting with full disaster recovery, and we connect each integration to the live system — the interfaces are already built and tested against the real formats. Go-live by T plus three months, on your milestone schedule; it's configuration and integration, not a redesign."

**"Your matrix shows a sample — does it cover the whole RFP?"**
"The live matrix samples the highest-value requirements across all seventeen sections so you can verify them today. The complete, row-by-row response to Annexure I and Annexure II — every requirement marked Readily Available, Customisable, or as appropriate — is in our written technical proposal. No row is left blank."

**"The RFP asks for microservices; this looks like one application."**
"It's a *modular* application — each module has isolated data and clean service interfaces, so any module can be extracted into its own service with no business-logic rewrite. We chose microservices-ready over microservices-overengineered to cut your cost and risk. If you require fully separated services, we deploy them that way."

**"How will you handle Aadhaar legally?"**
"Through a licensed AUA/KUA partner in production. The e-KYC flow and UIDAI data handling are already built. We store only hashed identifiers and the last four digits — data minimisation by design, as the DPDP Act requires."

**"Data residency and localisation?"**
"All personal data stays in an India region. No external service holds personal data. We support bring-your-own-key encryption, and data is encrypted both in transit and at rest."

**"How do you satisfy RBI / NABARD / CERT-In?"**
"Tamper-evident hash-chained audit — which you can verify live — role-based access, MFA, privileged-access management, a software bill of materials, and an independent VAPT plus a CERT-In empanelled audit in our delivery plan. Source-code escrow protects continuity."

**"Are you large enough to support a state apex bank?"**
"We're a focused Kerala startup — local, fast, and accountable, with defined SLAs, tiered support, and two L2 resources on-site at the Bank. You get the senior engineers who built the system, and source-code escrow guarantees continuity regardless."

**"What happens when DPDP Rules change or the MeitY stack launches?"**
"Regulatory-change management is included free for five years from go-live. The MeitY consent-stack adapter is already wired — we switch it on when the Government publishes it, with no re-quote."

**"Why shouldn't we just pick the lowest price?"**
"Because this is a quality-led, seventy-thirty evaluation, and you're choosing a five-year compliance partner for a bank. We lead on quality with a working system, and our efficiency keeps our price fair — that's best value and lowest risk."
